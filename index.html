A session runs about eight to ten minutes and mixes the drills for your level. Quick note is a single cold note for between lessons. It always counts as a first note, your purest score. When you sing, sing the letter name itself on the pitch, like "D" on D. Turn off the silent switch.

Walk the wheel plays each slice in turn, as a note or a major chord, in the octave set above. Neighbors on the wheel are a fifth apart and share similar colors. Notes a half step apart sit almost opposite each other. Every octave of a note has the same color, and the colors never change.

One note at a time, heard in different octaves with its color, name and octave. Hum or sing along. If two of your picked notes are ones you mix up, a contrast round plays them back and forth.

First note counts only notes with nothing before them: session openers and quick notes. Comeback counts the first blind block after a break of three hours or more, so it shows what sticks. Sung on pitch means within a quarter of a half step, any octave. Answer time is your typical time on correct answers; the best adult learners in research average about two seconds. Misses lean shows whether your wrong answers tend to land sharp or flat.
