Walk the wheel plays each slice in turn, as a note or a major chord, in the octave set above. Neighbors on the wheel are a fifth apart and share similar colors. Notes a half step apart sit almost opposite each other. Every octave of a note has the same color, and the colors never change.

Match

Cold open is the four notes at the start of each session plus your quick notes, heard with nothing before them and answered with no reveal until the end. It's the score to watch. Comeback counts the first blind block after a break of three hours or more, so it shows what sticks. Sung on pitch means within a quarter of a half step, any octave. Answer time is your typical time on correct answers; the best adult learners in research average about two seconds. Sung within a half step counts on it, close and neighbor. Sung misses lean shows whether your near misses when singing tend to land sharp or flat.
